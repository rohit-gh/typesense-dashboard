# Typesense Dashboard

## Users

People who run Typesense nodes: backend engineers, search owners, and operators. They arrive already knowing the cluster exists. They work at a desk, often with a second monitor, and they switch between this UI and API docs or a terminal. Sessions are task-shaped: connect, inspect, change one thing, leave.

## Product Purpose

Give a connected Typesense node a clear place to manage collections, documents, search, synonyms, curations, keys, aliases, and cluster health. The dashboard does not sell Typesense. It operates it.

## Brand Personality

BaseStation: editorial, warm, unhurried. Serif, paper, and ink. Learners who move quickly, without SaaS chrome.

## Voice

Plain, specific, and calm. Name the object and the consequence. Prefer “Delete alias staging” over “Confirm”. No jokes in destructive paths. Domain words the audience already knows (collection, synonym, curation, node) stay; internal leftovers (“Nb documents”, “Field is required”) do not.

## Product Principles

1. One primary action per screen. Secondary work is disclosed, not competing.
2. Brand lives in type, color, and restraint. Familiar dashboard patterns stay: sidebar, table, form, dialog.
3. Dark is the default room. Light is the inverted page, not a different product.
4. Tables, JSON, and IDs are the content. Decoration that does not help scanning is removed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body and controls meet 4.5:1 contrast on both themes. Keyboard focus is visible. Icon-only controls have names. Do not rely on color alone for success or failure. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
